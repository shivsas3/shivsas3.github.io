Self-Locking Tape Measure — Reverse Engineering & Part Definition

Independent design project: product teardown, dimensional measurement, and 2D/3D part definition.
Onshape Dial Caliper GD&T 8 Components
  • Reverse-engineered a self-locking tape measure by fully disassembling the product and dimensioning all 8 components with a dial caliper.
  • Derived nominal dimensions and assigned dimensional tolerances at each mating interface to maintain the clearances and fits required for the mechanism to function.
  • Created a 2D detail drawing for each component, 3D modeled each in Onshape, and built the full assembly.
